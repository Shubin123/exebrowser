Play Haunted mirror maze puzzle (Undead) online Works — plays right here, unlimited puzzles

Guide updated October 2026 · Runs via WebAssembly (native port)

Undead is a logic puzzle set in a haunted room full of diagonal mirrors. Every empty square holds a ghost, a vampire or a zombie. Vampires only show when seen directly, ghosts only in a mirror, and zombies always. Numbers round the edge count what you see looking in from that spot, and the total of each monster is given. How to play

  • Fill every square that is not a mirror with a ghost, a vampire or a zombie.
  • The counts at the top give the total of each monster type.
  • A number on the edge says how many monsters are visible looking into the grid from there, following the line of sight as it bounces off mirrors.
  • Zombies are always visible. Vampires are visible only before the line of sight hits a mirror. Ghosts are visible only after it has been reflected at least once.
  • Mirrors reflect on both sides, and a monster seen twice along one path is counted twice.

Sizes and difficulty

The Type menu offers 4x4 and 5x5 grids at Easy, Normal and Tricky, and 7x7 at Easy and Normal. Custom sets any size and difficulty.

Solving tips

A clue of 0 is a strong start: every square on that path before the first mirror cannot be a vampire or zombie, and every square after it cannot be a ghost or zombie. Paths that never hit a mirror can only show vampires and zombies, so ghosts there are invisible. Pencil marks are useful for listing the two or three monsters a square could still be. Keep an eye on the totals at the top; they grey out when a type is complete.

Where this puzzle comes from

Undead is part of Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection, a set of about forty logic puzzles written by Simon Tatham and contributors and released under the MIT licence. Every grid is generated on the spot, so the supply never runs out, and for the grid-logic puzzles the generator makes sure each has exactly one solution, reachable by reasoning rather than guessing. The version here is the collection's own code compiled to WebAssembly; the Game menu can save a puzzle to a file and load it again, and the Type menu changes the size and difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I see a ghost I placed in a straight row?

Ghosts only show up when the line of sight has bounced off at least one mirror. Looked at directly, they are invisible and do not add to the count.

Who invented this puzzle?

David Millar invented it under the name Haunted Mirror Maze. Undead is the version in Simon Tatham's collection, contributed by Steffen Bauer.

Is Undead free to play here?

Yes, with no limit on the number of puzzles. Undead is open-source software under the MIT licence, and every grid is generated fresh when you press New game.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. A tap works as a click and the puzzle resizes to fit the screen. Puzzles whose controls use the right mouse button are easier with a mouse.
