About SimAnt

Wright based SimAnt on E. O. Wilson's research into ant behaviour, and the game includes a short course on how real colonies work. There are three ways to play: a quick game on a single patch, the full game across the whole yard and house, and an experimental mode for building your own conditions. Ants lay scent trails others follow, and the colony's caste balance is yours to set.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of SimAnt as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
