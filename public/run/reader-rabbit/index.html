About Reader Rabbit

Reader Rabbit began on the Apple II in 1984 and became one of the best-selling children's software series of the 1990s, with a dozen sequels. This is the 1989 DOS version with EGA graphics, aimed at ages three to six. Its activities are the Sorter, the Word Train, the Labeler and Word Match. Everything is driven by the arrow keys and Enter, so it works well on a touch screen with the buttons below.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of Reader Rabbit as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
