About Nibbles

NIBBLES.BAS was Gorillas' companion in the QBasic samples folder: a short program anyone could open, read and change, and many people learned to program that way. Two players can share the keyboard, one on the arrows and one on W, A, S and D. Each level adds walls, and the skill setting from 1 to 100 sets the speed.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of Nibbles as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
