About Paratrooper

Paratrooper is one of the first widely-copied PC games. It runs in CGA's cyan-and-magenta palette and plays in short, increasingly frantic rounds. Shooting a helicopter scores more than shooting a falling soldier; hitting a soldier's parachute sends him straight down, and he can crush the men already on the ground.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of Paratrooper as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
