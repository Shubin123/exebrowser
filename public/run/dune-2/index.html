About Dune II

Dune II introduced the building blocks of the genre: resource harvesting, a build tree that opens as you construct buildings, a fog of war over an unexplored map, and units that fight in real time. Westwood built Command & Conquer on the same design three years later. Units are ordered one at a time, which was standard in 1992; select a unit, click an order such as Move or Attack, then click the target.

Tips for getting started

Build a second Spice Refinery and a couple of harvesters early; credits limit everything else. Concrete slabs under buildings stop them crumbling. Keep harvesters off open sand when a worm sign appears.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of Dune II as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 10 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
