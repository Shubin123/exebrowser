Play Light Up puzzle (Light Up) online Works — plays right here, unlimited puzzles

Guide updated October 2026 · Runs via WebAssembly (native port)

Light Up is a placement puzzle: put light bulbs on a grid of white and black squares so that every white square is lit, while no two bulbs shine on each other. Numbers on the black squares say how many bulbs touch them. Nikoli publishes it as Akari. The puzzle is ready below; click a white square to place a bulb.

How to play

  • A bulb lights its own square and every square in a straight line up, down, left and right until a black square or the edge stops it.
  • Every white square must be lit.
  • No bulb may be lit by another bulb.
  • A numbered black square must have exactly that many bulbs directly above, below, left and right of it. Unnumbered black squares have no limit.

Bulbs that see each other and numbers that are wrong or cannot be met are shown in red.

Sizes and difficulty

Presets come at 7×7, 10×10 and 14×14 in easy, tricky and hard. Easy puzzles can be solved without guessing; hard ones may need some. Custom also sets the rough share of black squares and their symmetry.

Solving tips

Start with 4s (bulbs on all four sides) and with 0s (mark all neighbours as no bulb). A 3 against an edge or wall, or a 2 in a corner, is also fully forced. Then look for white squares that can only be lit from one place: if a square's row and column are all blocked or already marked, the bulb must go on the square itself. Mark no-bulb squares freely; it makes the remaining options obvious.

Where this puzzle comes from

Light Up is part of Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection, a set of about forty logic puzzles written by Simon Tatham and contributors and released under the MIT licence. Every grid is generated on the spot, so the supply never runs out, and for the grid-logic puzzles the generator makes sure each has exactly one solution, reachable by reasoning rather than guessing. The version here is the collection's own code compiled to WebAssembly; the Game menu can save a puzzle to a file and load it again, and the Type menu changes the size and difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

Can a bulb sit next to an unnumbered black square?

Yes. Only numbered black squares restrict how many bulbs are around them.

Do lit squares need to be lit by exactly one bulb?

No. A white square may be lit by several bulbs; the only ban is a bulb being lit by another bulb.

Is Light Up free to play here?

Yes, with no limit on the number of puzzles. Light Up is open-source software under the MIT licence, and every grid is generated fresh when you press New game.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. A tap works as a click and the puzzle resizes to fit the screen. Puzzles whose controls use the right mouse button are easier with a mouse.
