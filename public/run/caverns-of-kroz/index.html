About Caverns of Kroz II

Scott Miller wrote Kroz in Turbo Pascal and sold it through the shareware model that became Apogee's: a free episode to try, the rest by mail order. Every level is a single screen of walls, traps, keys, lava and treasure. Monsters move only when you do, which makes each screen a small puzzle about timing and whip charges as much as a fight.

How it runs here

This is the original DOS release of Caverns of Kroz II, unmodified, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
