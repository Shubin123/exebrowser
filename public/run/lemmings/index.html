About Lemmings

Lemmings began on the Amiga and was ported to almost everything, the DOS version included. Its 120 levels are split into four difficulty ratings, Fun, Tricky, Taxing and Mayhem, and each has a password so you can come back to it. Each level gives you a fixed number of each skill and a minimum number of lemmings to save. The puzzle is in spending them well: a blocker in the right place turns the whole crowd, and one builder too few means a fall.

Tips for getting started

Use P to pause and plan. The plus and minus icons change the release rate. Write down the level password shown after each success: it is how the game remembers your progress.
