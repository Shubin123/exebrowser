About Frontier: Elite II

Frontier modelled orbits and gravity properly, so a trip across a star system means accelerating, flipping round and braking, usually under time acceleration. The economy, military ranks and missions are far deeper than the original's. It was one of the most ambitious games of its time, and Braben's studio later made Elite Dangerous on the same idea.
