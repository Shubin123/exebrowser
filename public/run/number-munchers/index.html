About Number Munchers

MECC, the Minnesota Educational Computing Consortium, made Number Munchers for the Apple II in 1986 and brought this VGA DOS version out in 1990. Its five game types are Multiples, Factors, Primes, Equality and Inequality, and the difficulty climbs from third-grade arithmetic to work that still takes some thought. Every few levels a short cartoon plays as a reward. Eat a wrong number and you lose a life.

Tips for getting started

Pick the game type and grade level from the Muncher Menu. Watch the edges of the grid: Troggles enter from the sides, and the warning "Troggle!" flashes just before one comes in.
