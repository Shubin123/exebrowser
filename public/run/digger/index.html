About Digger

Windmill Software, a small Toronto firm, sold Digger on a self-booting disk with a copy-protection scheme of its own, and the game was everywhere on CGA PCs in the mid-80s. A Nobbin turns into a Hobbin that can dig after a while. Collect eight emeralds in a row for a bonus, and eat the cherry to turn the tables on the monsters for a few seconds.

How it runs here

This is the DOS release of Digger as preserved in the Internet Archive, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
